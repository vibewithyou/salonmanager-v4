<product_requirements>
The user requested a comprehensive, multi-tenant SalonManager application. The core problem to solve is providing a modern digital management system for salons.

**Key functionalities and features include:**
*   **Core Concept**: Individual salon pages with booking and galleries, dedicated customer, stylist, and admin areas, online payments, a shop, inventory management, and AI features. The app should be PWA-ready, multi-language, and GDPR compliant.
*   **Design & UX**: Responsive design (mobile-optimized, touch-friendly, dark/light mode), a design editor per salon, interactive navigation, multi-language support (DE, EN, FR, IT, TR, AR, RU), modern icons, and PWA capabilities.
*   **Salon & Stylist Management**: Customizable salon pages (with link & QR), gallery management, promotions/news area, stylist profiles (services, availability, ratings), team roles (Admin, Stylist, Apprentice), schedule management, and a favorites function for clients.
*   **Appointment Logic & Booking**: Booking with stylist selection, drag & drop calendar for admin/stylists, recurring appointments, email/SMS reminders, client-stylist chat, optional Google Calendar sync, stylist confirmation, and gallery-based hairstyle selection during booking.
*   **Customer Area**: Client profiles (name, email, image, language, favorite services), GDPR data export/account deletion, appointment overview, loyalty card, feedback/ratings, push notifications, and onboarding tours.
*   **Shop, Vouchers & Finance**: Product sales per salon, payment processing (Stripe, PayPal, SEPA, optional Klarna/Apple Pay), digital voucher sales/redemption, automatic PDF invoices, refund processing, financial analytics (daily/weekly/monthly export), and a subscription system for salons.
*   **AI Modules**: AI-driven appointment and hairstyle suggestions, AI-simulated hairstyle try-on, AI chatbot for customer queries, and sentiment/trend analysis of reviews.
*   **Location Search & Map**: Interactive Leaflet-based map with salon markers, filter functions (location, price, rating, availability), live appointment display on map, and auto-generated QR codes per salon.
*   **Admin & Backend**: Role and rights management (Admin, Salon Owner, Stylist, Customer), bulk data export/import, backup management, system monitoring, and API access management.
*   **Security & Legal**: GDPR compliance (export, deletion, consent, privacy policy per salon), impressum & terms/conditions generator, 2-Factor Authentication for staff, login logs/IP tracking, and hosting security (SSL, UFW/Fail2Ban, Cloudflare).
